Wounds

Every strike hits a real body part. Cuts and thrusts bleed until they clot or you bind them, and a fight can be lost to blood loss alone.

Footwork

Circle, sidestep a committed swing, or get behind a guard: a block only stops what is in front of it.

Crippling

Leg wounds slow you and stop you dodging. Arm wounds make your swings slower and weaker. Mace blows to the head daze, and scalp cuts blind you with blood.

Weapons

Nineteen to choose from. Blades bleed, axes and polearms hit hardest, thrusting points reach furthest, and hammers and maces break bone and push through a block. Heavy weapons swing slower and slow your feet, and each weapon's body thrust has its own speed and damage.
